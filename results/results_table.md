# Lunar Lander results

Independent evaluation on the **plain** (unwrapped) `LunarLander-v3` environment,
deterministic PPO actions, **20 episodes × 3 eval seeds** (`2000`, `2001`, `2002`)
for each training seed. Env seeds are `eval_seed * 10000 + episode_index`, so they
do not overlap the training-time eval seeds 1000–1029.

**Landing rate** is the fraction of episodes that ended with the +100 at-rest bonus.
Landing, crash, and timeout are mutually exclusive: a lander that comes to rest on
step 1000 is counted as landed, not as a timeout. Std is the sample standard
deviation (`ddof=1`) of all episode returns pooled under the variant.

**Mean training time** is the average wall-clock time per seed from
`results/training_times.csv`. Runs were trained in three parallel batches on an
8-core machine: (1) the four Trainer seed-0/1 runs (`plain_s0/s1`,
`time_penalty_s0/s1`); (2) the six Reward Hacker runs (`hack` and `fix`, seeds
0–2); (3) the two Trainer seed-2 runs (`plain_s2`, `time_penalty_s2`). These
times reflect that load and are **not a speed comparison** between variants.

## Notes

- `time_penalty` is pulled down by seed 1, which had not converged by 1M steps (mean 182.50, landing 73.3%, crash 20.0%, timeout 6.7%). Seeds 0 and 2 landed on every evaluated episode (means 274.02 and 271.40).
- `hack` hovers on the plain env: seeds 0 and 2 almost always time out near 1000 steps; seed 1 is mixed (landing 58.3%, timeout 38.3%, crash 3.3%). The large aggregate std (128.86) is mostly the gap between the two hovering seeds (means 43.26 and −177.37) and the mixed seed (86.17).
- `fix` recovers landing: 96.7–100% at-rest landings across three seeds, mean return in the mid-260s, comparable to the baseline.

Source: `results/analysis_eval_all.csv` and `results/training_times.csv`, generated by `src/analysis/evaluate.py`.
