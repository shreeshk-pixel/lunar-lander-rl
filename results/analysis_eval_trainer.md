# Lunar Lander evaluation results

Evaluated on plain Gymnasium `LunarLander-v3` (no reward wrappers) with deterministic PPO actions.

Each model was run for **20 episodes** on each of **3 eval seeds** (`[2000, 2001, 2002]`). Training seeds that share a label are reported individually and then aggregated.

**Landing rate (return ≥ 200)** is the fraction of episodes whose episodic return is ≥ 200 (Gymnasium's official solved threshold).

**Landing rate (at-rest +100)** is the fraction of episodes that terminated with a final-step reward of +100 (the lander came to rest). This is the Trainer's landing definition.

**Crash rate** is the fraction of episodes that terminated with a final-step reward of -100 (crash or out of bounds).

**Timeout rate** is the fraction of episodes cut off at the 1000-step time limit (`truncated=True`). A high timeout rate is the signature of a hovering policy.

## Comparison with the Trainer's reported eval

The Trainer evaluated each model for **30 deterministic episodes** on env seeds **1000–1029** (`src/evaluate.py --seed 1000 --episodes 30`). Landing was defined as the episode **terminating with a final-step reward of +100** (at-rest bonus). The reported standard deviations match `numpy.std` with `ddof=0` (population std) in `results/<run>_eval.json`. Wall-clock times in those claims match `results/<run>_train.json`. `results/training_times.csv` was not present when this check started; it later appeared with only the Reward Hacker's `hack`/`fix` runs, not the Trainer's `plain`/`time_penalty` times.

This independent check used a **different eval-seed set**: seeds `[2000, 2001, 2002]`, 20 episodes each (**60 episodes per model**). Env seeds are `eval_seed * 10000 + episode_index`, so there is no overlap with 1000–1029. Std below is the **sample** standard deviation (`ddof=1`). Both landing definitions are shown; the comparison uses the Trainer's at-rest (+100) rate.

A discrepancy is flagged when `|Δ mean|` exceeds ~1× the Trainer's reported std, or when the at-rest landing rates differ by more than 0.1.

`plain_s0`, `plain_s1` and `time_penalty_s0` agree with the Trainer: mean differences are well under one reported standard deviation, and landing-rate differences are under 0.1.

**`time_penalty_s1` is flagged on landing rate.** The at-rest landing rate here is 0.733 vs the Trainer's 0.600 (Δ = 0.133 > 0.1). The mean return (182.50 vs 169.9) is still well inside the Trainer's ±101.7 std, so the mean is not flagged. Other differences on this seed, not used for the formal flag but consistent with a policy that had not converged by 1M steps (as the Trainer noted): crash rate matches at 0.200, but timeout rate is 0.067 here vs 0.200 in the Trainer's eval, and mean length is 362.2 vs 444.1. Different eval seeds plus high variance are a sufficient explanation; the mean itself is not in conflict.
