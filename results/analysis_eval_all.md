# Lunar Lander evaluation results

Evaluated on plain Gymnasium `LunarLander-v3` (no reward wrappers) with deterministic PPO actions.

Each model was run for **20 episodes** on each of **3 eval seeds** (`[2000, 2001, 2002]`). Training seeds that share a label are reported individually and then aggregated.

**Landing rate (return ≥ 200)** is the fraction of episodes whose episodic return is ≥ 200 (Gymnasium's official solved threshold).

**Landing rate (at-rest +100)** is the fraction of episodes that terminated with a final-step reward of +100 (the lander came to rest). This is the Trainer's landing definition.

**Crash rate**, **timeout rate** and **landing rate (at-rest +100)** are mutually exclusive and should sum to 100% (plus **other** if an episode fits none of those). A lander that comes to rest on step 1000 (Gymnasium may set both `terminated` and `truncated`) is counted as landed, not as a timeout. Raw `truncated=True` is still recorded in the CSV as `truncated_rate` and can be higher than the exclusive timeout rate.

**Crash rate** is the fraction of episodes that ended with a final-step reward of -100 (crash or out of bounds), including a crash on the time-limit step.

**Timeout rate** is the fraction of episodes cut off at the 1000-step time limit without an at-rest or crash outcome. A high timeout rate is the signature of a hovering policy.

## Comparison with the Trainer's and Reward Hacker's reported evals

Both agents evaluated each model for **30 deterministic episodes** on env seeds **1000–1029** using `src/evaluate.py` (landing = terminated with final-step reward +100). Their reported standard deviations match `numpy.std` with `ddof=0` (population std) in `results/<run>_eval.json`.

This independent check used a **different eval-seed set**: seeds `[2000, 2001, 2002]`, 20 episodes each (**60 episodes per model**). Env seeds are `eval_seed * 10000 + episode_index`, so there is no overlap with 1000–1029. Std below is the **sample** standard deviation (`ddof=1`). The comparison uses the at-rest (+100) landing rate.

Outcome categories here are **mutually exclusive**: landed-at-rest, crashed, or timed out (they summed to 100% on every row; `other` was 0). A lander that comes to rest on step 1000 (Gymnasium may set both `terminated` and `truncated`) is counted as landed, not as a timeout.

The shared `src/evaluate.py` can double-count that case. In the Reward Hacker's `results/hack_s1_eval.json`, episode seed 1005 has `terminated=True`, `truncated=True`, `landed=True`, length 1000, return 103.3, so their published `landing_rate` 0.600 + `truncated_rate` 0.433 = 1.033. The exclusive split of that 30-episode eval would be land 0.600, timeout 0.400, crash 0.000. No other published eval JSON had a landed-and-truncated overlap.

A discrepancy is flagged when `|Δ mean|` exceeds ~1× the agent's reported std, or when the at-rest landing rates differ by more than 0.1.

**`time_penalty_s1` is flagged on landing rate.** The at-rest landing rate here is 0.733 vs the Trainer's 0.600 (Δ = 0.133 > 0.1). The mean return (182.50 vs 169.9) is still well inside the Trainer's ±101.7 std, so the mean is not flagged. Crash rate matches at 0.200; timeout is 0.067 here vs 0.200 in the Trainer's eval, and mean length is 362.2 vs 444.1. That is consistent with a policy that had not converged by 1M steps (as the Trainer noted), plus different eval seeds.
